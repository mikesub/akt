## MusicBrainz contact

`akt@mksb.me` is this project's contact address. It has two uses only:

- MusicBrainz: its User-Agent (`src/http.js`) is `akt/0.1 ( akt@mksb.me )`, as MusicBrainz asks. A User-Agent without a contact is throttled as anonymous, in a bucket shared with every other such client.
- The site's public contact, for corrections and missing playlists: the contact line at the top of `docs/index.html` and in its footer, at the owner's request.

It is not the owner's personal address; never use it for any other service.
